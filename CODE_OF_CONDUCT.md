# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Participate with
respect, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and focus discussion
on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats,
unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, or sustained
disruption of community spaces.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates these standards.
Report concerns privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting or
by contacting an organization owner. Reports will be handled respectfully and
as confidentially as practical.
